THE SYSTEM

Three layers, each owning what it is actually best at. JESTA holds revenue truth. Shopify holds site-level commercial metrics. GA4 holds attribution and behaviour at channel and campaign level. The allocation layer multiplies them together so every channel figure carries JESTA revenue behind it.

08

WHY THIS IS MORE ROBUST

Seven structural gains. The only new spend is engineering time on collection quality.
  • Revenue truth sits above the platform. JESTA is the ledger, so no channel figure can drift from what finance recognises. Shopify net becomes an input, not the ceiling.
  • Credit is distributed, not assigned. Every Shopify model hands 100% of an order to one touchpoint. GA4 splits by measured contribution, so channels that open journeys stop reading as zero.
  • Direct is exposed as a data problem. Switch between the three Shopify models above and direct barely moves. It only collapses under GA4, because it was never a channel.
  • Rates gain two levels of resolution. CVR and NCA % move from one site number to a number per channel and per campaign, which is where the decisions actually get made.
  • Cross-device journeys are joined. Shopify treats a phone browse and a desktop purchase as two unrelated visitors. GA4 stitches them into one path.
  • The funnel becomes diagnosable. Four store-wide checkpoints become eight segmentable ones, so a CVR decline can be located rather than just observed.
  • Nothing is replaced and nothing is licensed. GA4 runs on roughly 70% of Shopify stores against 13% for paid attribution platforms. Existing KPI definitions are untouched.
